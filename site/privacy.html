Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This privacy notice describes how the BACK2U Creator Dashboard handles information for its private, owner-operated creator analytics service and the planned TikTok connection.

1. Operator and contact

The operator is the owner of the BACK2U Creator Dashboard project. For general enquiries, use the project contact channel. The issue tracker is public, so please never post sensitive information there; request a private contact method first for confidential privacy enquiries.

2. Information processed

  • Connected creator accounts: platform-provided account identifiers and basic profile information, where authorized.
  • Video and analytics data: video IDs, titles or captions, posting dates, links, thumbnail references, views, likes, comments, shares and other metrics when available.
  • TikTok authorization: if TikTok login is enabled, access/refresh tokens and authorization metadata necessary to maintain an authorized connection. This feature is being tested and is not yet a public production service.
  • Technical security data: necessary authentication/session cookies and technical request details handled by hosting services.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Data is used to display analytics for authorized creator accounts, compare content performance, maintain requested platform connections and protect account access. Authorized TikTok account access is initiated by the account owner through TikTok's consent screen. The applicable legal basis depends on the specific use and role of the operator; this private creator tool is not offered for general public registration.

4. Where information is processed

The dashboard is hosted on Netlify. Authorized metrics can be prepared via Google Sheets and Google Apps Script and sent to private server-side storage at Netlify. Existing exports may also involve the creator's connected analytics providers, including Windsor.ai. The TikTok connection, once configured, stores authorization credentials in restricted server-side storage, not in published HTML files. Providers may process information in different countries under their own privacy and security arrangements.

5. Sharing

Information is handled by the services necessary to operate the dashboard and connected integrations. The project does not intentionally sell creator analytics data or use it for third-party advertising. Data that is publicly visible on TikTok, Instagram or YouTube remains governed by the respective platform.

6. Retention and withdrawal

Analytics data, snapshots and connection credentials may remain stored while the dashboard and the associated integration are used. Authorization to a connected platform can be withdrawn in the platform's account settings. A withdrawn authorization prevents further authorized access; previously saved dashboard copies may require a separate deletion action by the operator.

7. Cookies and security

The dashboard uses a necessary HTTP-only session cookie to keep its owner logged in. Temporary authorization state cookies protect the TikTok login flow. These are functional and security-related, not advertising cookies. The dashboard requires authentication for private analytics views.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, a data subject may request information, correction, deletion or restriction of personal data, as well as withdraw a consent where it is the basis for processing. Contact the operator using the project contact channel above; do not post private details in public GitHub issues.

9. Changes

This notice may be updated when the dashboard or connected APIs change. The update date at the top identifies the current version.

Important: This is an operational notice for the current private project. Before offering the service publicly, the operator must review it for applicable legal obligations, including complete operator identity, a suitable confidential contact channel and any country-specific disclosures.
