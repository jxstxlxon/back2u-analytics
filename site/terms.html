Terms of Service

Last updated: November 11, 2026

These terms apply to the BACK2U Creator Dashboard, an independent, owner-operated web dashboard for viewing creator-content analytics from connected social media platforms.

1. Purpose and access

BACK2U Creator Dashboard is currently a private creator analytics project, not a public social network or a service that accepts general user registrations. Access to the analytics dashboard is restricted by authentication. The publicly accessible informational pages may be viewed without logging in.

2. Authorized connections

Only accounts that the account owner is authorized to manage may be connected. When TikTok Login is enabled, the person authorizing access will be shown the requested permissions by TikTok. The intended TikTok integration uses user.info.basic and video.list to identify the connected account and read public video information and available statistics. The integration is not intended to post, edit or delete TikTok content.

3. Data and statistics

Information from TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram is shown for analysis and convenience. Platform metrics may be delayed, incomplete or defined differently. The dashboard does not guarantee that figures are accurate, available continuously or comparable across platforms.

4. Platform and provider terms

Your use of TikTok, Google/YouTube and Instagram remains governed by their own terms. BACK2U is an independent project and is not endorsed by or affiliated with TikTok, Google, YouTube, Meta or Instagram.

5. Security and availability

Do not share dashboard credentials or use the service to access another person's account without permission. Features, integrations or the dashboard itself may change or become unavailable if a connected provider changes access rules or an API becomes unavailable.

6. Privacy

Use of connected accounts and technical data is explained in our Privacy Policy.

7. Contact

For project questions, use the BACK2U project issue tracker. Issues are public: do not post passwords, tokens, emails or other personal information. For a confidential inquiry, request a private contact method without sharing sensitive data in the public issue.

Scope: This document describes the current private creator-tool setup. A publicly offered production service may require expanded terms and operator/contact disclosures.
